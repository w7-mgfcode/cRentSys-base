# Historical Versioning & Baseline Changelog

> **Module**: Version History & Baseline Evolution  
> **Application**: cRentSys (LocalRent / CentRent)

---

## Baseline Release: Version 3.0 (2013) — `v3-original_2013`

### Summary of Features in Baseline:
- **Guided Customer Booking**: 5-step rental funnel with conflict detection and automated day calculations.
- **Dynamic Surcharges**: Operating hours integration with location-based pickup/delivery pricing.
- **Rich Dispatch Calendars**: Monthly grid matrix visualizer powered by `wz_tooltip.js`.
- **Contract Engine**: Instant Microsoft Word RTF contract generation (`contractor.php`).
- **Financial Module**: Monthly turnover auditing with historic 20% Hungarian VAT and 50% investor share split.
- **Fleet CRM**: Vehicle category and physical asset inventory CRUD.
